Iceland

Discover the Land of Fire and Ice

About Iceland

Iceland is a beautiful island country in the North Atlantic Ocean, famous for its dramatic landscapes, volcanic mountains, glaciers, waterfalls and natural hot springs. It is a perfect destination for travelers who love nature and adventure.

From the spectacular Northern Lights to the famous Blue Lagoon, Iceland offers unforgettable experiences throughout the year. Visitors can explore charming towns, discover unique natural attractions and enjoy the peaceful beauty of the countryside.

Day 01 – Arrival and Reykjavik

Arrive in Iceland and explore Reykjavik, the country's capital. Visit Hallgrimskirkja Church, walk through the colorful streets, discover local restaurants and enjoy the city's welcoming atmosphere.

Day 02 – The Golden Circle

Discover Iceland's famous Golden Circle route. Visit Thingvellir National Park, watch the impressive Strokkur geyser erupt and admire the beautiful Gullfoss waterfall.

Day 03 – Waterfalls and Relaxation

Explore Iceland's spectacular waterfalls and natural scenery. Enjoy a relaxing experience at a geothermal hot spring before preparing for your departure.
